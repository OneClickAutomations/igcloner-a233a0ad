clone modes

Three ways to learn from what works.

Each mode gives you a different strategic lens on a competitor's best content.

A1 · Steal the Frame

Same image,
stronger words.

Keep the exact visual style, composition, and colors. IGCloner rewrites only the caption, hook, and message to fit your brand and audience.

  • Preserves the viral visual format exactly
  • Rewrites caption with your brand voice
  • Generates 3 hook variations to test
A2 · Reframe It

Same format,
your niche.

Adapt the proven structure, hook style, and content arc to a completely different topic or audience — zero visual overlap.

  • Remaps the topic to your industry
  • Keeps the proven format and pacing
  • Full script ready to record
A3 · Counter-Angle

Opposite take,
same virality.

Flip the original's premise. Disagree with its claim, invert its hook, or challenge its approach — controversially and constructively.

  • Finds the counternarrative angle
  • Builds in debate-bait hooks
  • Higher comment/share potential
